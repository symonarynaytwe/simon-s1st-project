Meet the Founder

Arynaytwe Simeon

CEO & Founder, Simeon footwear

Welcome to Simeon footwear! Beyond coding and exploring modern web technologies, I am an avid sports enthusiast who loves football, fitness, and tracking the latest global design trends. My journey into the world of footwear started with a personal passion for sneaker culture—admiring the unique blends of comfort, engineering, and style from elite global brands.

Frustrated by how difficult it was to find genuine, premium sneakers at reasonable rates locally, I was motivated to start this business. I wanted to create a transparent marketplace specifically built for Ugandan sneakerheads. For me, nothing brings greater joy or fulfillment than seeing the satisfaction of a happy customer stepping out in style with a perfect pair of kicks from our store.
